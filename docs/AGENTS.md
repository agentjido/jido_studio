# Documentation instructions

## Docs Kata

Use [the index](README.md) to find the document that owns a subject.
Collect unreviewed material in [the inbox](inbox/README.md). Record its
source, date, and status. Review its claims before you use it as evidence.

### Place documents

- Keep instructions for current tasks in guides and current system facts in
  reference. Record accepted decisions with their reasons.
- Keep active plans and decision roadmaps at their existing paths.
- Keep historical material in archive with its version and source. Mark which
  behavior was observed and which behavior came from code, tests, or specs.
- Keep legacy product contracts and source links at their existing paths.
  Record exceptions in the inbox log.

### Maintain documents

- Give each new document a title, status, review date, and evidence links.
- Distinguish current behavior, proposed work, and accepted decisions.
- Link related documents. Update the document that owns a subject.
- Preserve existing links, assets, attribution, and upstream references.
- Check affected links, the index, and the intake log after a change.
